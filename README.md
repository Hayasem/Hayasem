## 🇺🇸 About me

I'm a Junior Development Analyst at **Staf Sistemas**, where I build and maintain software for Brazilian municipal governments on the **Betha Suite** platform.

My daily work involves writing **Groovy / BFC-Script** report scripts, business validation rules and **JasperReports** templates across the Tax (*Tributos*), Protocol, Legal (*Procuradoria*) and Education modules — serving municipalities such as Dourados, Maracaju, Bodoquena, Nova Andradina and Sapezal. I also work with relational databases (**SQL Server / T-SQL**, PostgreSQL) and payment integrations using the **Banco do Brasil Cobranças/PIX APIs**.

Outside of that, I develop **REST APIs with Java and Spring Boot** — my main personal focus and the direction I want my career to grow in.

- 🎓 Technologist degree in Systems Analysis and Development — Universidade Insted (2023–2025)
- 🌱 Currently deepening: Spring Boot, JPA/Hibernate, automated testing and clean architecture
- 🗣️ Portuguese (native) · English (fluent, certified)
